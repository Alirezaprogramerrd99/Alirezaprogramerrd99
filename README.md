### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am currently an **MSc Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** candidate at the **University of Hull, UK** 🇬🇧, specializing in **LLMs, RAG Systems, and LLMOps**. I hold a BSc in Computer Engineering from **Shahid Beheshti University** 🇮🇷 (Ranked Top 10).

My passion lies in bridging the gap between research and production—building scalable AI infrastructures using **AWS SageMaker**, **ZenML**, and **FastAPI**. When I'm not training models, I enjoy playing the **guitar** 🎸 and listening to **classical music** 🎻.
