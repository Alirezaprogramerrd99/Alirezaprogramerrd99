## 🔬 About Me

I am currently an **MSc Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** candidate at the **University of Hull, UK** 🇬🇧, specializing in **Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Agentic AI, and LLMOps**. I hold a BSc in Computer Engineering from **Shahid Beheshti University** 🇮🇷 (Top-10 ranked).

My work focuses on the **intersection of research and production**: designing **multi-agent AI systems**, **RAG pipelines**, and **end-to-end LLM lifecycles** that are scalable, observable, and deployable. I actively work with frameworks such as **ZenML**, **LangChain**, and **FastAPI**, deploying models via **AWS SageMaker** and **local inference stacks** such as **Ollama**, **Qwen**, and **LoRA / PEFT**.

My research interests include:
- Agentic AI and multi-agent coordination
- Trustworthy and efficient small language models
- RAG-based factual grounding & hallucination mitigation
- Evaluation, monitoring, and safety of deployed LLMs

Outside of AI, I play the **guitar** 🎸 and enjoy **classical music** 🎻.
